Dragon Snack Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Dragon Snack Dash is operated by Jason Lam under the name Larango Studios (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains information practices for the iOS game and our support website.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores your best score, preferences, and selected age range locally on your device. It asks for an age range, rather than your name or exact date of birth. Our game code does not send this saved age-range selection or best score to a Larango Studios server.

The game does not require an account and has no player chat or public profile. It does not ask you to provide contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or precise location.

2. Rewarded advertising

The game uses Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and ironSource Exchange to provide optional rewarded ads. Completing an eligible ad can allow you to continue a round. You may choose Play Again instead of watching an ad.

Advertising services receive information directly from your device. Depending on the service and applicable restrictions, this can include your IP address, installation or device identifiers, device and app information, advertising interactions, reward events, and technical performance information.

These services use information to deliver and operate advertising, process rewards, measure ad delivery, prevent fraud, and maintain service security. Advertising information may be processed by Unity, its ironSource affiliates, and their service providers and advertising partners subject to applicable restrictions.

3. Children and advertising settings

Dragon Snack Dash welcomes players of different ages, including children. We configure the advertising SDK to apply child-directed treatment to every player, regardless of the selected age range. We also send signals indicating that advertising consent has not been granted and that sale or sharing should be restricted.

Our intended advertising experience is contextual, rather than based on a player's behavior across other apps and websites. The game does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. These settings do not mean that advertising services collect no information: limited identifiers and operational information can still be processed.

We do not offer features for children to publicly share personal information. Parents and guardians should contact us themselves rather than asking a child to send personal information by email.

4. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use this information to respond, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests. Email is provided through Google Gmail. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information about a child.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, generally removes its local data; device backups may retain copies.

We delete ordinary support correspondence within 90 days after the issue is resolved, unless continued retention is necessary to handle an ongoing request or meet a legal obligation. Unnecessary personal information received from a child will be deleted when identified.

Advertising services control retention of information they collect. Unity states that, for child-directed advertising, installation-related app interaction data is retained for up to 12 months and transaction records for up to 180 days for billing and fraud prevention. Additional information is available in Unity's policy below.

6. Privacy requests and parental rights

You, or a parent or guardian acting for a child, may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict further collection where applicable. Email larangostudios@gmail.com with the subject “Dragon Snack Dash Privacy Request.”

We may request information reasonably necessary to verify the request. We do not maintain player accounts and may be unable to identify advertising records from a name or email address alone. Where appropriate, we will help route requests to the relevant provider.

Unity also provides privacy controls through the privacy icon in supported ads and accepts privacy inquiries at DPO@unity3d.com. You can stop future information collection through the game by no longer using it and deleting it. Deleting the game does not automatically delete records already held by advertising providers.

7. Providers, website hosting, and international processing

Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Their policies explain their processing, recipients, safeguards, and privacy-request options:

  • Unity advertising services privacy policy — Unity Ads, LevelPlay, and related ironSource services.
  • Google privacy policy — Gmail support correspondence.
  • GitHub privacy statement — website hosting.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, or contact forms to it. GitHub may collect technical access information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting.

8. Security and policy changes

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, but no electronic service is completely secure. We will update this policy when our practices change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before making changes.
